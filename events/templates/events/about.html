About CampusConnect

Discover, connect and participate in campus events.

What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a campus event management platform designed to help students discover and participate in events happening around their campus.

The platform provides a central place where users can explore upcoming events, view event details and register for events that interest them.

Registered users can also create and manage their own events, making it easier for students to organise and share activities with the campus community.

Discover

Find academic, social, sports, technology and other events happening around campus.

Connect

Connect with other students and become part of the campus community through shared activities.

Participate

Register for events and take part in activities that make campus life more enjoyable.

Our Purpose

CampusConnect aims to make campus events easier to discover, organise and participate in by bringing students and campus activities together in one place.
